How to play Imposter Game

What is this game?

One secret word is given to all civilians. The imposter(s) do not know the word — they only see the category (or a hint, depending on difficulty). Take turns describing the word, then vote out who you think is bluffing.

Civilians win

Vote out every imposter before they blend in. In reverse mode the goal flips: keep the imposter alive.

Imposters win

Survive the votes and reach parity with the civilians, then guess the secret word to steal the round.
